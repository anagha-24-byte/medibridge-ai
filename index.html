When to Seek Emergency Care Immediately

Never use this or any AI tool if you are experiencing severe or life-threatening symptoms:

  • Crushing chest pain, pressure, or tightness spreading to arm, jaw, or back
  • Sudden severe shortness of breath or inability to catch your breath
  • Sudden numbness, facial drooping, or slurred speech (Signs of Stroke - FAST)
  • Uncontrolled heavy bleeding, sudden confusion, or loss of consciousness

Our Medical Safety & Privacy Pledge

How we protect patients, support doctors, and keep your data safe

🛡️ 1. No Diagnosis & No Prescriptions

MediBridge AI is designed solely for health literacy. It empowers patients with clear communication tools for discussions with licensed physicians. It cannot replace clinical judgment.

🔒 2. Privacy & Data Control

Your medical text and reports belong to you. We require simple sign-in with only your Name and Mobile number, scope records to your account, and give you full power to delete your history at any time.

🚨 3. Red-Flag Emergency Protocols

Our systems actively detect words related to cardiac, respiratory, or neurologic emergencies to direct users to emergency services (112 / 108) without delay.
